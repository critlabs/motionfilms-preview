02/WHO WE ARE

Founded on a single, guiding purpose.

RIYADH · KINGDOM OF SAUDI ARABIA

Motion Films was founded to engage cultural dialogue through cinema. Partnering with Action Films, LLC, our journey began by bringing together a collective of people with a true, genuine passion for the craft. Our team in Riyadh is backed by a 25-year legacy of regional production.

Over the last two decades, our founding partners at Action Films have collaborated with government institutions, multinational corporations, and industry leaders across the GCC, earning a reputation for both technical and cinematic excellence.

Now, under Saudi leadership, Motion Films draws on that deep well of experience to produce work that creates a lasting impact — crafting narratives that spark cultural dialogue and visualise the abstract, intangible parts of the Saudi and Arab experience.

While delivering premium content to our clients is a core pillar of our business, our commitment to the art of cinema itself runs just as deep. Our award-winning Arabic short film, Al Majhool, reflects that passion — and that our commitment to storytelling extends into cinema itself.

04/OUR MISSION

Democratising the cinematic arts.

Based in Riyadh, Motion Films is deeply committed to supporting Saudi Arabia's rapidly expanding film industry. As the Kingdom's creative sectors reach a global audience like never before, we deliver premium production services in alignment with Vision 2030.

We believe in breaking down traditional barriers to entry. Recognising that active audience engagement is key to cinema's growth in Saudi Arabia, we are building a talent-development initiative — outstanding audience-submitted scripts will be chosen for development and production.

This programme is also a hub for on-set mentorship. By giving aspiring Saudi filmmakers hands-on, practical training, we work to build and strengthen the local industry's infrastructure from the ground up.

Crucially, this vision for the future is backed by profound industry expertise.
